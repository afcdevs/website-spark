The Story

We got tired of paying for sleeping bots

Spark started in May 2026 when three friends realized the bots they paid $12/mo for went offline more often than their own laptops. We built the host we wanted: simple, fast, and run by people who answer messages.

Everything runs on AMD Epyc nodes with NVMe drives, dedicated per server — not a shared $5 VPS pretending to be "premium". Our panel is Pterodactyl, the same one the big hosts use, because we'd rather spend time making hosting good than reinventing a control panel.

What that means for you

  • Price stays the price — what you see is what you pay, every month.
  • We answer — support is a real person in our Discord, not a ticket queue.
  • You can move — free migration, and a refund if the first 48 hours aren't right.
  • We keep it running — monitored around the clock, because your community depends on it.
